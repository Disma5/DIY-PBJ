FOOD SHOULD NOT BE A BARRIER TO CREATIVITY.

The PBJ Social Program is a grassroots DIY project dedicated to making simple food freely available at music festivals, art events, and other community gatherings.

We provide the ingredients and a place to make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich. No payment. No application. No questions.

The idea is simple:

COME HUNGRY.
MAKE A PBJ.
SHARE ONE IF YOU CAN.
GET BACK TO CREATING.

OUR MISSION

Music festivals can be incredible places for connection, experimentation, and creativity. They can also be expensive, physically demanding environments where basic needs can easily get overlooked.

We believe that a small act of practical generosity can make a difference.

The PBJ Social Program exists to create temporary, welcoming food stations where anyone can grab the ingredients for a simple meal and continue participating in the community around them.

THIS IS A DIY SOCIAL PROGRAM.

It isn't about charity as a spectacle. It's about neighbors helping neighbors and making sure hunger doesn't have to interrupt the experience.
